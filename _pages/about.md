## Professional Bio

Alberto Alcocer Jr. is a data scientist, software engineer, and bioinformatics researcher based in Houston, Texas. With a foundation spanning computational biology, full-stack development, and applied machine learning, he bridges the gap between scientific research and real-world software systems. Currently pursuing his studies at the University of Houston–Clear Lake (UHCL) in Data Science, Alberto brings both academic rigor and hands-on technical depth to every project he takes on.

His work ranges from building AI-powered applications to conducting original bioinformatics research. He developed **Laila**, an automated AI voice translation and dubbing system designed to ease language barriers through real-time speaker voice cloning. He created **NeuroView**, a 3D brain visualization tool that makes neurological data more accessible and interpretable. His **AI-Powered Nutritional Analysis Application** applies machine learning to dietary data, while his ongoing **Yeast ORFan** project uses bioinformatics pipelines and machine learning to classify uncharacterized hydrolase enzymes in the yeast genome — research with direct implications for enzyme discovery and computational biology.

Professionally, Alberto has worked as an IT Support Technician, where he developed a strong operational foundation in systems administration, credential management, and technical troubleshooting. His technical repertoire includes Python, Java, SQL, and a range of data science and bioinformatics tools, making him equally at home in a research lab or a software engineering environment.

Alberto is deeply interested in the intersection of natural language processing and biology — particularly how transformer-based models can be applied to genomic sequences, protein function prediction, and bioinformatics workflows. He actively explores research opportunities at the frontier of AI and computational biology.
